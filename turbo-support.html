Turbo Lane Racer — Support

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Turbo Lane Racer is a free endless lane-dodger made by Syed Abbas. Questions or problems? Email quickbillapp.support@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

How do I play?

Tap PLAY, then drag to steer your car across the lanes. Dodge the traffic, grab coins to build your score, and pick up fuel cans — when your fuel runs out, the run ends. One crash also ends the run, so thread carefully!

How is my best score saved?

Your best score is saved on your device only, so it's there next time you play. There are no accounts and no sign-in. Deleting the app deletes the saved score.

Does the game collect my data?

No. The game has no analytics, no ads, and no servers — nothing leaves your phone. See the privacy policy for details.

Why is there no sound?

ersion 1.0 ships without sound effects. Sound is planned for a future update.

The game isn't working. What should I try?

Close the app fully and reopen it. If that doesn't help, restart your phone. Still stuck? Email us with your device model and iOS version and we'll help.
