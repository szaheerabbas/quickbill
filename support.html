Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. QuickBill works without signing up, and your data stays on your phone.

How do I back up my invoices?

Open Settings → Backup & restore → Export backup, then save the file to Files, iCloud Drive or your email. Do this regularly, because your invoices live only on your phone.

How do I move to a new phone?

On the old phone, export a backup. Move the file to the new phone, then open Settings → Backup & restore → Import backup and choose Replace all.

Why am I not getting payment reminders?

Turn on Settings → Payment reminders and allow notifications when your phone asks. Reminders only arrive for invoices that have a due date and aren’t marked Paid, and they arrive at 9:00 AM. If you turned notifications off earlier, switch them back on in your phone’s Settings → Notifications → QuickBill.

Can I invoice in different currencies?

Yes. Each invoice keeps the currency it was created in. Change the currency for new invoices in Settings → Currency. The Dashboard shows totals for each currency separately.

How do I add my VAT, GST or sales tax number?

Open Settings → Tax, choose the tax name, enter your number and default rate, and tap Save tax settings.

How do I delete my data?

Delete invoices one by one from their screens, use Settings → Reset app to clear your business profile and logo, or delete the app to remove everything. Nothing is stored on our servers.
