Privacy Policy

Last updated October 18, 2026

QuickBill keeps your invoices, customers and settings on your phone. There’s no account to create, and we never receive your data.

We don’t run servers for the app, so we can’t see, sell or share what you enter. The details are below.

What the app stores on your phone

QuickBill saves what you enter so it’s there the next time you open the app: your invoices and the customer details on them (names, emails, phone numbers, addresses and tax numbers), your business profile and logo, your tax and payment settings, your chosen currency and your reminder choices. All of this is kept in the app’s private storage on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. QuickBill doesn’t send your information to us or to anyone else. The app has no accounts, advertising, tracking or analytics, and it doesn’t include third-party tools that collect data about you.

Permissions the app asks for

Photos
Only when you tap Upload logo. The app reads the one image you choose and saves a copy inside the app. It doesn’t browse or upload your photo library.
Notifications
Only if you turn on payment reminders. Reminders are scheduled on your phone and appear at the time you chose. They aren’t sent from a server.

When you share something

Some actions send information out of the app, but only when you start them and only to where you choose:

  • Sending an invoice PDF opens your phone’s share sheet. The app you pick (Mail, Messages, WhatsApp and so on) receives the PDF and handles it under its own privacy policy.
  • Exporting a backup creates a file with all your invoices, including customer details. You decide where it’s saved, so keep it somewhere private.
  • A payment link on an invoice opens in the browser or app of whoever taps it, and that site’s own policy applies.

Phone backups

Apple and Google can include an app’s data when they back up your phone (for example in an iCloud backup), depending on your phone’s settings. That is handled by them under their policies, and we don’t receive it.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you remove it. You can delete an invoice from its screen, clear your business profile and logo with Settings → Reset app, or delete the app, which removes everything it stored. Backup files you exported are yours to delete.

Your customers’ information

If you enter details about your customers, you’re responsible for them. QuickBill only stores them on your phone for you. Please use them only for invoicing, and follow the privacy rules that apply where you work.

Children

QuickBill is a business tool and isn’t aimed at children. We don’t knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give people the right to see, correct or delete personal data that a company holds about them. Because we don’t receive or hold any of your data, there is nothing on our side to look up or delete. Your data is on your device and under your control. If you have a question, email us.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects something you agreed to, we’ll say so in the app’s update notes.
